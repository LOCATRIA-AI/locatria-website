—
Newest – Oldest lead age
Why two numbers for lead age? Cohort results cover a date range:
  • Oldest leads (from period start): Used to evaluate appointment timing. If even your oldest leads have had less time than you usually take to book a meeting, it is too early to judge.
  • Newest leads (from period end): Used before concluding "no clients yet." We don't judge the absence of a signed client until every lead has had your usual signing window.
